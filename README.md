# Pf2e Awesome Macros For Players
Overview: A repo with more useful macros for the Pathfinder2e system, this time for players to use!

Contents: \
1.) Enhanced Recall Knowledge Macro for rolling recall knowledge, asking which skill to use and what question you want to ask. 
It will then roll the skill and give the result to the GM. It will also give helpful hints to the GM to answer better. 
Also , if you have any special lores, it will suggest the best one to use based on your current skill modifiers.

2.) Macro for rolling a random new character with various options to enable/disable. 
(Example: You can choose to roll a random character with or without a class, with or without a background, etc.)

3.) Easy exploration for updating or mass updating people exploration activities. Easy to stop and start any activity. 

4.) Monster Hunter for ranger to "Hunt Prey" and call the enhanced recall knowledge macro.

5.) Magus Analysis for magus to recover their Spellstrike call the enhanced recall knowledge macro.

6.) Known Weaknesses for investigator to call the enhanced recall knowledge macro when using devise-a-stratagem.

7.) Combat assessment macro to do a strike and then call the enhanced recall knowledge macro to assess the target on a hit.

8.) Mastermind Rogue macro for setting off-guard on success or critical success of recall knowledge.

9.) Flurry of Blows for monk to call the flurry of blows action.
 
10.) Hunted Shot for ranger to perform two ranged strikes against their hunted prey, progressing MAP normally and combining damage on hits.
 
11.) Open Party Stash for players to quickly open the shared party stash / inventory tab with one click.
 
12.) Instructive Strike for thaumaturge to make a strike and trigger Enhanced Recall Knowledge on a hit, gaining a +2 circumstance bonus on a critical hit.
 
13.) Double Slice for dual-wielding characters to make two melee strikes using one-handed weapons, defaulting agile to the second weapon with non-agile penalty calculations and combined damage on hits.
 
14.) Twin Feint for rogues to make two melee strikes with different weapons against the same target, automatically treating the target as off-guard for the second attack (enabling Sneak Attack) and applying MAP normally.
 
15.) Reset Movement to instantly revert token movement during combat back to turn origin with one click, clearing recorded waypoint history and ruler lines.

Macros are automatically organized in Foundry into dedicated subfolders under **PF2e Awesome Macros For Players**:
- **Recall Knowledge**: Enhanced Recall Knowledge, Monster Hunter, Known Weaknesses, Combat Assessment, Magus's Analysis, Mastermind Rogue's Recall, Instructive Strike.
- **Combat**: Flurry of Blows, Hunted Shot, Double Slice, Twin Feint, Reset Movement.
- **General / Utility**: Roll Random Character, Easy Exploration, Open Party Stash.
